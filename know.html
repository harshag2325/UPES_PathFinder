Pathfinder

About the project

Find your way around campus.

UPES Pathfinder is designed to help students, faculty, and visitors navigate the university campus more easily and explore it with confidence.

Why this project?

Finding a destination on a large university campus can be confusing, especially for new students and visitors. This project aims to make campus navigation more interactive and convenient, helping people reach their destinations without getting lost.
